## Data security

### In an era where data breaches and privacy concerns are paramount, IOMETE's self-hosted architecture provides enterprises with unprecedented control and security over their data assets, ensuring data stays within your trust perimeter at all times.

### Complete data sovereignty

Unlike SaaS solutions where your data resides in vendor-controlled environments, IOMETE's self-hosted platform ensures your data never leaves your security perimeter. This means:

- Complete control over data location and movement
- Integration with existing security infrastructure
- No dependency on external security measures

### Minimized data movement

Self-hosted architecture significantly reduces data movement risks compared to SaaS solutions:

- Data stays within your infrastructure
- Reduced exposure to transfer vulnerabilities
- Lower risk of unauthorized access during transit
