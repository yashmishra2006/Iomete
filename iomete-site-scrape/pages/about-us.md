OUR INSIGHT

# Cloud-SaaS is not the right delivery model to manage and extract value from large data sets in a cost-efficient manner.

While cloud-SaaS data platforms such as Snowflake and Databricks can be convenient for smaller organizations just starting on their data journey, cloud-SaaS is per definition one-size-fits-all and therefore rigid.

It also leads to vendor lock-in and unnecessary data movement. This drives up cost and may results in data leaks. This is why over the past four years, we’ve built a data platform that addresses these serious issues based on real-world user feedback.

WHY WE’VE BUILT IOMETE

## This is why over the past four years, we’ve built a data platform that addresses these serious issues based on real-world user feedback.

IOMETE is a self-hosted platform, meaning it’s installed in the customer’s trust perimeter. It runs on-premises, as well as on cloud.

Due to its open nature it is extremely flexible and can be tailored to unique organizational leads. It is significantly more cost-efficient (> 2x) than cloud-SAAS alternatives. We’d love for you to try it out.

Who we are

## IOMETE is built by engineers, for engineers. Our team is fully remote, spanning seven different countries.

Our journey began in the trenches of the likes of Amazon, Google, Meta and Uber where we experienced firsthand the challenges and limitations of existing SaaS data platforms.

At IOMETE, we understand that at the heart of great engineering is the drive to solve problems. That’s why IOMETE is not just a platform, we consider ourselves an extension of your engineering team.

Designed to empower enterprise data teams, IOMETE facilitates a level of customization and control that mainstream solutions fail to offer.
