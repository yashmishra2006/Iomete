RETHINKING DATA PLATFORMS

## Design principles

### In today’s enterprise landscape, organizations are struggling with data platforms built for yesterday's challenges. The usual suspects started out 10-15 years ago, carry significant technical debt and have transitioned from nimble engineering teams into bloated sales armies. These aging architectures trap organizations in inflexible deployments, escalating costs, and vendor lock-in. IOMETE represents a fundamental break from the past – a modern, self-hosted data lakehouse platform built from the ground up for today’s enterprise needs.

OUR DESIGN PRINCIPLES

1

#### Complete data sovereignty

You pay them boatloads of money and then they lock you in...

We believe organizations should have absolute control over their data infrastructure. Our self-hosted architecture ensures you maintain full ownership of your data, security, and deployment choices – whether on-premises, in the cloud, or in hybrid environments.

2

#### Enterprise-grade security by design

In 2023, over 300 million individuals were affected by publicly reported data breaches, with the average data breach costing companies $4.45 million. In SaaS environments petabytes of data are moved between environments. This increases the chances of data leaks. At IOMETE security isn't an afterthought but a foundational element of our self-hosted architecture. Your data never leaves your trust perimeter. From military-grade encryption to fine-grained access controls and comprehensive audit logging, every component is built to meet the strictest enterprise security requirements.

TRUST PERIMETER

3

#### True deployment flexibility

Modern enterprises need flexibility without compromise. Our platform runs anywhere – from on-premises data centers to any cloud provider, including private clouds. This flexibility ensures you can deploy where it makes most sense for your business, without vendor lock-in.

4

#### Cost optimization at scale

We believe in maximizing the value of your infrastructure investments. Our architecture enables you to leverage existing resources, capture cloud provider discounts, and optimize costs through spot instances – often delivering 2-3x cost efficiency compared to traditional solutions.

UNIFIED IOMETE PLATFORM – CONSISTENT EXPERIENCE ACROSS ENVIRONMENTS

5

#### Unified data infrastructure

Complex data environments shouldn't mean complex management. Our platform unifies data lakes and warehouses into a single, powerful lakehouse architecture, simplifying management while maintaining enterprise-grade performance and reliability.

UNIFIED DATA INFRASTRUCTURE

6

#### Performance without compromise

Scale shouldn't mean sacrificing performance. Our modern, cloud-native architecture delivers consistent performance from gigabytes to petabytes, ensuring your critical workloads run efficiently at any scale.

7

#### Enterprise integration by default

We believe in enhancing, not replacing, your existing investments. Our platform seamlessly integrates with your enterprise systems, from identity management to monitoring tools, ensuring consistent security and operational practices.

8

#### Partnership in innovation

We don’t just provide software; we become an extension of your team. Our commitment to customer success means working closely with you to solve your unique challenges and accelerate your data initiatives.
