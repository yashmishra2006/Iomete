# Power BI - Connecting to IOMETE

## Choose a Connection Method

IOMETE supports three ways to connect Power BI. Choose the guide that matches the connector and protocol you want to use.

| Method | Connector | Guide

| Arrow Flight (recommended) | IOMETE Arrow Flight ODBC connector | Arrow Flight

| Thrift | Power BI's built-in Spark connector | Thrift

| Thrift ODBC | Simba Spark ODBC driver | Thrift ODBC
